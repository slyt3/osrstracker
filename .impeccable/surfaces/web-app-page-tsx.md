## Scope & mode

Lead surface: the landing (`web/app/page.tsx`), mode **Persuade**. The chosen
world is durable and carries across the Operate surfaces (board, item,
watchlist, journal, craft, pricing) via `globals.css`.

## Audience, job, action

OSRS merchers alt-tabbed from the game, deciding what to buy/sell now. Primary
action: **Open the flip board**. Proof: a live wall of real after-tax,
liquidity-weighted flips — not a claim, the working mechanism.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A flip terminal carved in runestone. Every live figure is an incised
rune that **kindles gold only when the profit is real and fillable**; thin or
losing values stay cold stone. It refuses the two ruts: a re-skin of the
in-game Grand Exchange panel (a costume of the tool) and the dark fintech
trading terminal (the category's predictable opposite).

OWN-WORLD: Ground is raking-lit granite/slate — warm near-black stone with real
chiselled texture and bevelled recesses, one directional light modelling every
incision. Primary carried colour is incised **coin-gold** (headings, rules,
live figures); a single **alert-red** reserved for losses; **cold unlit gold**
for dormant values. A two-ink economy on stone. Headings: Cinzel inscriptional
capitals cut in (inset shadow). Body: Spectral. Figures: Spline Sans Mono as
machined numerals. Controls are carved stone keys with real depress travel.
**Glow-by-law:** only a profitable AND liquid flip gets the gold enchant-glow.
Recognisable with content stripped: dark carved stone + glowing gold runic
numerals + incised caps.

STORY: A merchant lands on a wall of live runestone figures, sees the top flips
literally glowing gold (real, fillable margin after tax), understands in one
glance that the lit ones are the move, and clicks into the full board.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed carved-stone console. A brass feed-strip across the
top (LIVE · updated HH:MM:SS · 60s). Left: an inscriptional headline cut into
stone — the key phrase gold-incised — one line of Spectral subcopy, two carved
stone keys (Open the board / Pricing). Dominant below: a live runestone tally of
the top ~10 flips — item, buy, sell, −tax, +margin, ROI, vol — margins glowing
gold where profitable+liquid, cold where thin. Primary action gold-lit and
unmistakable. The section below begins as a ruled stone section, not more hero.

FORM: Runestone Engraving / lithic inscription; candidate 5 of 7 on the grounded
resonance list (assigned by the roll); seed key 89095626. Raises: +two-ink
economy & condensed inscriptional caps & ruled billing-block footer (noir
one-sheet); +glow-by-law meaning (CRT arcade); +kindling light on interaction
(plankton); +single committed raking light (indoor-sun); +carved controls with
